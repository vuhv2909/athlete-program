Pain rule

Neu mot bai lam dau ro rang o goi, lung duoi, co, hoac vai: dung bai do, giam tai, doi bien the, hoac bo qua hom do. Muc tieu phase nay la dep dang hon, khong phai co gang gang suc.

General reminders

Van giu suc manh toi thieu de khong yeu di, nhung trong tam 8-12 tuan toi la lean + posture. Sau khi gon hon va dung dang tot hon, luc do quay lai phase strong/athletic se hop ly hon nhieu.
